My Story

I used to be the person who couldn't stick to anything.

Three years ago, I was exhausted, overwhelmed, and convinced that healthy living just wasn't for someone like me. I'd tried every program, every diet, every "30-day challenge." They all worked for about a week.

What nobody told me was that consistency isn't about willpower — it's about systems. It's about having someone in your corner who understands your real life, not an idealized version of it.
